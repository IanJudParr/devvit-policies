# Privacy Policy — fixture-picks

Last updated: 5 October 2026

fixture-picks is a free prediction game that runs inside Reddit communities. It is
built by an individual developer ("I", "me") using Reddit's Devvit platform.

## What the app stores
- Your Reddit username and user ID, as provided by Reddit when you use the app.
- Your picks, your points, and your position on the community leaderboard.
- Short-lived cached football fixture data (not personal).

Nothing else. I do not ask for or store your name, email, location, or any
payment information.

## Where it is stored
All app data is kept in Reddit's own Devvit storage, scoped to each community
that installs the app. It is not copied to any server I run or to any third party.
Reddit's own privacy policy applies to how Reddit handles your data.

## What is shared
- Nothing about you is shared with third parties.
- The app fetches fixture and result data from football-data.org. These requests
  are made by the app's server and contain no information about you.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or cookies are added by this app.

## How long data is kept
Picks and scores are kept for the football season to run the leaderboard, then
deleted or archived at the start of the next season. You can ask for your data
to be deleted at any time (see below).

## Your rights
You can ask what the app holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted,
or object to its use. Contact me using the details below. Under UK data
protection law you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

## Children
The app is intended for Reddit users, who must meet Reddit's minimum age.
It does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will change. Material changes will be
noted in the app's description.
